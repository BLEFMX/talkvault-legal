# TalkVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

TalkVault respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use TalkVault.

## 1. Information We May Access

When you connect a social media account, TalkVault may receive information that the platform makes available through its authorized APIs, such as account identifiers, profile information, and information needed to publish or manage content.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide and operate TalkVault's social media automation features, including managing and publishing content through connected accounts.

## 3. Third-Party Platforms

TalkVault may use services and APIs provided by third-party platforms such as TikTok. Those platforms may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain the service, or comply with applicable obligations.

## 6. Your Choices

You may disconnect authorized social media accounts or stop using TalkVault at any time.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the TalkVault team through the contact method associated with the service.
